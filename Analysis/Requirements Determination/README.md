## Summary of Core Requirements

### Functional Requirements
1. **User Authentication & Profiles:** Job seekers and employers can register, log in, and manage their profiles.
2. **Location-Based Job Search:** Job seekers can browse and filter nearby job openings by geographic location, category, and keywords.
3. **Job Posting Management:** Employers can create, edit, and close job listings with location details and requirements.
4. **Application & Direct Messaging:** Job seekers can apply to listings and exchange direct messages with employers regarding open positions.

### Nonfunctional Requirements
* **Operational:** Accessible via modern desktop and mobile web browsers.
* **Performance:** Search queries and message delivery respond within 2–3 seconds under normal load.
* **Security:** Passwords are encrypted, and role-based access control separates applicant and employer permissions.
